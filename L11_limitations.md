1. Correlation does not imply causation possibilities...

   a) Confounder:
      ice cream sales -/-> shark attacks
   
   b) Reverse causality:
      umbrellas -/-> rain
   
   c) Pure coincidence: 
      https://www.tylervigen.com/spurious-correlations
      (e.g., Google searches for 'how much wood can a woodchuck chuck' 
      correlates with Kerosene used in Venezuela @ r=0.894)
